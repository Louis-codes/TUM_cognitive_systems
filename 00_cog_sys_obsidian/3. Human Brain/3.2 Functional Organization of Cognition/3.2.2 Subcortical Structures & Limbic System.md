3.2.2 Subcortical Structures & Limbic System


Cognition is not restricted to the cortex. Several subcortical structures contribute essential functions.

| Structure     | German         | Description and function                                                                                     |
| ------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Basal ganglia | Basalganglien  | Group of nuclei (Kerne, clusters of neurons) beneath the cerebral cortex; mainly related to the motor system: planning, selection, execution and learning of actions |
| Hippocampus   |                | Sea horse-shaped region (Greek *hippokampus*: horse + sea monster); formation of episodic long-term memories   |
| Amygdala      |                | Group of nuclei analyzing the emotional or motivational significance of stimuli                               |

The hippocampus and the amygdala are part of the limbic system, one of the oldest structures in the brain.
