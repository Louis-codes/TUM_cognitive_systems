3.4 Distinctive Features of the Human Brain


It is a central question of brain research which properties make the human brain and human cognition stand out among other species.

### Recent findings

| Feature                          | Finding                                                                             |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Neuron morphology                | Pyramidal neurons in the human cortex have the most elaborate and spine-rich dendritic trees |
| Electrical properties of neurons | Human cortical pyramidal neurons have a very low membrane capacity, which significantly enhances signal transmission |
| Sequence processing              | Humans have superior capabilities of learning sequences of stimuli                   |

Ghirlanda, Lind & Enquist (2017) show that non-human species have only a limited capacity to discriminate ordered sequences of stimuli; errors persist after thousands of trials in tasks humans learn to near perfection within tens of trials.

### Brain size and intelligence

- Absolute brain size does not determine intelligence: elephants and whales have much larger brains than humans.
- The encephalization quotient (EQ) relates brain size to the size expected for a species of the same taxon. Humans reach ≈ 7.4–7.8, bottlenose dolphins ≈ 5.3, chimpanzees ≈ 2.2–2.5.
- The number of cortical neurons is a better predictor: human ≈ 11.5 billion, elephant ≈ 11 billion, chimpanzee ≈ 6.2 billion.

### Common myths

| Myth                                          | Reality                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Only ten percent of the brain are used        | Every part is used, but not all parts at once (which would result in epilepsy)  |
| The brain does not produce new neurons in adults | In certain brain areas new neurons are produced throughout life                |
| Brain size is related to intelligence         | Elephants and whales have much larger brains than humans                        |
