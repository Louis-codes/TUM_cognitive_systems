### Levels of modeling
After Herz et al. (2006), single-neuron models span five levels of detail:

| Level | Model type                    | Description                                                                            |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I     | Detailed compartmental models | Capture detailed morphology from anatomical reconstructions; geometry discretized into compartments, each modeled as a point neuron |
| II    | Reduced compartmental models  | Only a few dendritic compartments, lower computational complexity, higher analytical tractability |
| III   | Single-compartment models     | Ignore morphology; detailed spiking point neurons capture ion currents and spike generation |
| IV    | Cascade models                | Purely functional, represent abstract computations without biological detail            |
| V     | Black-box models              | No computational function; neuron as input/output system with defined statistic behavior |
