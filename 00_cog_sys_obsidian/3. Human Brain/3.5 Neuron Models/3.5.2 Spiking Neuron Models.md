3.5.2 Spiking Neuron Models


Spiking neurons emit impulses that correspond to the spikes emitted by biological neurons.

### Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF, 1907)

- Information processing in biological neurons is largely driven by ionic currents accumulated in the soma (Zellkörper).
- The flow of these currents is modeled with functionally equivalent electrical circuits (ionic currents represented as electrical currents).
- The LIF neuron is comprised of a simple RC circuit driven by an external current; it emits a spike once the membrane potential crosses the threshold $\vartheta$.
- It is the first spiking neuron model and still of high relevance today, mainly because of its computational efficiency.

### Extensions and alternatives

| Model                                       | Characteristic                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adaptive exponential integrate-and-fire     | Extended LIF (Brette & Gerstner 2005) that can simulate different firing patterns of biological neurons |
| Hodgkin-Huxley (1952)                       | Basis of most detailed spiking neuron models and of modern neural modeling and simulation |
| Izhikevich model                            | Efficient spiking model used in large-scale simulations                          |
