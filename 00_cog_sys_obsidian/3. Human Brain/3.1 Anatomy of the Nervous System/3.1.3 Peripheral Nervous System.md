3.1.3 Peripheral Nervous System


The peripheral nervous system (Peripheres Nervensystem, PNS) transmits signals between sensory organs, muscles and internal organs and the CNS.

### Autonomic Nervous System (Vegetatives Nervensystem)

Responsible for homeostasis (self-regulation) and operates largely unconsciously. It includes two antagonist subsystems.

| Subsystem       | German           | Role                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sympathetic     | Sympathikus      | Connection of internal organs to the brain; prepares the organism for stress (increased heart rate, blood flow to muscles, sweating) |
| Parasympathetic | Parasympathikus  | Mainly cranial nerves (Hirnnerven) and lumbar spinal nerves (Rückenmarksnerven); sets the body to resting state, increases digestive functions |
